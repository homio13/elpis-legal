# Elpis Support

日本語版は [こちら]({{ site.baseurl }}/support/)。

If something is not working, or you have a request, let us know through the form
below.

**[Contact form](https://forms.gle/8kfrTeD7yTGLdTM96)**

Elpis is built and run by one person, so a reply may take a few days. For bug
reports, telling us **which device you are using** (iPhone or Android, and the
model) and **what you were doing at the time** makes it much easier to track down.

## Frequently asked questions

### I have missed several days of recording

**That is fine.** Your monster will not disappear. A day with no record stays a
day with no record — we do not fill it in with zeros or averages.

Not recording is itself information about how that day went.

### I am changing phones. Can I move my data?

**Yes, if you have linked an account.**

Before you switch, on your current phone go to **Settings > Data > Continue with
Apple** (or Google). Once linked, you can restore on the new phone from
**Settings > Data > Transfer data from another device**.

**If you have not linked an account, your data never leaves the device.**
Deleting the app or resetting the phone means the records cannot be recovered.

### I tried to transfer and my records disappeared

If the Apple or Google account you tried to link was already connected to a
different Elpis account, you **switch** to that account instead. Records made on
this device before the switch are not carried over.

**A confirmation screen appears before the switch happens.** Stopping there keeps
your current data as it is.

### How do I cancel Elpis Plus?

**Cancel in your store's settings. You cannot cancel inside the app.**

- iPhone: Settings app > your Apple Account > Subscriptions
- Android: Google Play Store > menu > Subscriptions

Cancelling does not affect the monster you are raising — **you can still raise it
all the way to graduation.** Elpis Plus becomes relevant again at your next egg.

### How do I delete my account?

Go to **Settings > Data > Delete account**. This works even if you have never
linked an account.

**Deletion cannot be undone.** Records, monsters, your collection, and the
furniture in your room all go.

**One thing to watch: deleting your account does not cancel your subscription.**
Cancel in the store first, then delete the account.

### My Apple Health / Health Connect data is not coming through

Check the permission on your device.

- iPhone: Settings app > Privacy & Security > Health > Elpis
- Android: Health Connect app > App permissions > Elpis

Elpis reads only four things: **resting heart rate, step count, sleep, and active
energy**. It never writes anything back to Health.

### Screen time is not filled in automatically

**It cannot be read on iPhone.** Apple's Screen Time API does not allow apps to
read the values. On iPhone, enter it by hand on the record screen.

On Android, granting "Usage access" fills in your daily total automatically. We
do not collect app names or per-app usage.

### Can my monster die?

**No.** If you stop recording, or do not open the app for a while, your monster
waits.

A fully grown monster graduates, but it does not disappear. It stays in the room
and visits from time to time.
