# Delete your Elpis account

日本語版は [こちら]({{ site.baseurl }}/delete-account/)。

How to delete your account and related data in Elpis, a health-habit app (operated by Elpis).

---

## Option 1: Delete it in the app (immediate)

1. Open Elpis
2. Go to **Settings > Data > Delete account**
3. Confirm on the next screen

This works even if your account is not linked to Apple or Google. Deletion happens immediately.

## Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you have already removed the app, send a request through the
**[contact form](https://forms.gle/8kfrTeD7yTGLdTM96)** saying you want your account deleted.

- **If you linked Apple or Google**: include the email address of that Apple or Google account.
  We will find and delete the account, and let you know when it is done
- **If you never linked an account**: once the app is removed, we have nothing that identifies
  which account is yours, so we cannot delete it this way. **Please use Option 1 before removing the app**

We normally complete deletion within **14 days** of your request.

---

## What is deleted

- Your account identifier and anything received from a linked provider (Apple / Google)
- Your name, and your choice about the AI-written summary
- Every record (mood, habits, journal, screen time)
- Body data (heart rate, steps, sleep, active energy)
- Monsters, collection, materials, crafted items and their placement
- Weekly reports (including AI-written summaries)
- Stored subscription status

**Deletion cannot be undone.** We cannot restore data from backups.

## What is kept, and for how long

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| How the app is used (behavioural data; never the contents of your records) | Deleted automatically **14 months** after collection |
| Fault reports (crash circumstances and errors) | Deleted after roughly **90 days** (on Google's schedule) |

These sit with our analytics provider (Firebase / Google Analytics) and are not removed by
deleting your account. They expire automatically after the periods above.

## What deletion does not stop

**Deleting your account does not cancel your App Store or Google Play subscription (Elpis Plus).**
Cancel it in your store settings first.

- iPhone: Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions
- Android: Google Play Store > Menu > Subscriptions
