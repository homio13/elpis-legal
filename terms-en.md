carried over.** The App shows a confirmation screen before the switch; please
read it. Once you have switched, data that was not carried over cannot be
recovered.

## 7. About "effect stars"

The App shows, as a number of stars, how much each habit appears to affect mood.

**The data behind that display comes from one person — the developer — recording
for 6.5 years (2,329 days).** Of those, 1,854 days include a mood entry.

So the effect stars are:

- **An estimate drawn from a sample of one**, not a generalised statistic
- **Not an indication, and not a guarantee, that you will experience the same
  effect**
- Not offered as medical or scientific evidence

Treat them as one person's result — a starting point when choosing which habits
to try.
